Tattoo Price Estimator

AI-powered tattoo pricing calculator with detailed cost breakdowns

Overview

The Tattoo Price Estimator is an intelligent pricing tool that calculates fair tattoo costs based on size, style, complexity, placement, and artist experience. It helps artists provide accurate quotes and helps clients understand pricing factors.

Key Features

  • Size Calculation - Calculate square inches and apply industry pricing
  • Style Pricing - Different rates for realistic, traditional, geometric, etc.
  • Complexity Analysis - Factor in detail level, color vs. black & grey
  • Placement Adjustment - Account for difficulty based on body location
  • Multi-Session Quotes - Break down large pieces into sessions

How to Use

1. Enter Tattoo Details

Input size, style, and design complexity information.

2. Select Placement

Choose the body location to adjust for difficulty.

3. Set Your Rates

Configure hourly rate or per-square-inch pricing.

4. Get Quote

View detailed breakdown with session estimates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the AI pricing accurate for all regions?

The tool uses global industry averages. We recommend adjusting the hourly rate in settings to match your specific local market demand.

Can I save my generated designs?

Currently, designs are temporary. We recommend taking a screenshot or saving the image directly to your device for reference.

Does the tool account for cover-ups?

Yes, selecting 'High Complexity' and adding a placement note for scar tissue will adjust the time estimate accordingly.

What AI model powers the image studio?

We use a customized implementation of the Gemini Pro Vision model, optimized for anatomical artistic reference.

Can clients use this tool without an artist?

Yes, it is designed to help clients understand the variables that go into a professional quote, reducing price-shock during consultations.
