## 🎯 Overview

The Tattoo Price Estimator is a business utility for both artists and clients. It helps set realistic pricing expectations by calculating estimates based on several key factors, including design size, level of detail, color vs. black and grey, and body placement difficulty.

**Key Use Cases:**

- Initial client consultations
- Budget planning for large projects
- Standardizing shop pricing guidelines

**Target Audience:**

- Tattoo Artists
- Shop Managers
- New and experienced clients

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Variable Calculations** - Adjust size and complexity for dynamic pricing.
- ✅ **Style Presets** - Different logic for Fine Line, Traditional, Realism, etc.
- ✅ **Currency Support** - Localized calculations.
- ✅ **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- ✅ **Free & Open Source** - MIT licensed.
